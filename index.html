Understanding Catholic Life Coaching

It is essential to understand the distinct scope of life coaching so you can choose the best support for your situation:

What Coaching Focuses On:
  • Forward movement, actionable goals, and habits
  • Identifying unhelpful thought patterns and mindset blocks
  • Discernment in daily vocation, marriage, and work
Important Boundaries:
  • Does not diagnose or treat mental health conditions
  • Does not replace therapy or professional medical care
  • Is not spiritual direction (focuses on daily decisions rather than direct spiritual direction/theology)

Catholic life coaching works wonderfully alongside appropriate licensed medical care, therapy, and pastoral support when needed.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, straightforward answers to help you decide if coaching is right for you.

What is Catholic life coaching? ↓

Catholic life coaching is a supportive, goal-oriented partnership anchored in a Catholic understanding of the human person. It helps you gain clarity, overcome daily obstacles, align your thoughts with truth, and take practical steps toward fulfilling your vocation.

What is mindset coaching? ↓

Mindset coaching focuses on uncovering negative, automatic thought loops—such as perfectionism or anxiety—and replacing them with constructive, truth-based perspectives that foster peace and intentional decision-making.

How is coaching different from therapy? ↓

Therapy focuses on healing psychological trauma, mental health conditions, and past emotional wounds. Life coaching focuses on present habits, future goals, practical decision-making, and proactive growth.

Is coaching the same as spiritual direction? ↓

No. Spiritual direction explores your interior prayer life and discernment of God's voice directly. Coaching centers on your daily life, routines, thought habits, and practical goals, while staying fully aligned with Church teachings.

Why would I need a coach if God is the one who changes us? ↓

God frequently works through community, prudent mentors, and structured guidance. Coaching is a practical tool that helps you cooperate actively with His grace by clearing away mental noise and building virtuous habits.

What can I discuss during coaching? ↓

You can bring topics like balancing motherhood and work, managing household stress, improving communication in marriage, overcoming feelings of unworthiness, or discerning a new chapter of life.

How often will Sharon and I meet? ↓

Typically, clients meet weekly or bi-weekly depending on their selected package. This ensures enough time to implement action steps between sessions without feeling overwhelmed.

Which coaching option is right for me? ↓

We recommend starting with a free discovery call. During that conversation, we’ll assess your goals and determine whether an individual session, 6-week, or 12-week program best fits your season.

What happens during the free discovery call? ↓

It is a relaxed, 30-minute conversation. You share what you’re experiencing, ask any questions you have, and see if coaching feels like a helpful fit—with zero sales pressure.

What if I decide coaching is not the right fit? ↓

That is completely fine! There is never any obligation to enroll after a discovery call. My priority is ensuring you find the exact support you need.
