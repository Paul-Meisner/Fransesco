Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How is Catholic Life Coaching different from Therapy? ▼

Therapy primarily focuses on clinical mental health, healing deep past psychological trauma, and diagnosing conditions. Catholic life coaching focuses on where you are today and where God is inviting you to grow. We focus on actionable goals, spiritual alignment, mindset renewal, and practical strategies while honoring Church teaching.

How does coaching differ from Spiritual Direction? ▼

Spiritual direction focuses almost exclusively on your prayer life and relationship with God. Catholic life coaching includes spiritual integration, but also directly addresses practical life habits, time management, career decisions, mindsets, boundaries, and actionable personal development goals.

Are sessions conducted virtually or in person? ▼

Most sessions are held virtually via Zoom or by phone, allowing clients from anywhere across the country to participate in the comfort and privacy of their own homes.

What if I am returning to my faith or feel spiritually weak? ▼

You are welcome here exactly as you are! Sharon provides a warm, non-judgmental space. Whether you go to daily Mass or are just taking your first steps back into prayer, coaching meets you with gentle grace and encouragement.
