Clear Boundaries & Respect

Understanding Catholic Life Coaching

How coaching differs from therapy and spiritual direction, and how it can serve you.

01

Life Coaching

Focuses on practical goals, mindset, daily habits, decision-making, and forward movement. Helps mentally healthy individuals create order and alignment in life.

02

Psychotherapy

Focuses on healing past trauma, diagnosing, and treating mental health conditions (such as clinical anxiety or depression). Coaching does not replace clinical therapy or medical care.

03

Spiritual Direction

Focuses directly on prayer life, relationship with God, and discerning spiritual promptings. Coaching complements pastoral care but does not substitute spiritual direction.

Professional & Pastoral Ethics: Catholic life coaching is designed to complement professional medical care, psychological counseling, and pastoral support. If during our work together it appears that clinical therapy or formal spiritual direction would better serve your current needs, Sharon will respectfully encourage you to pursue those appropriate resources.
